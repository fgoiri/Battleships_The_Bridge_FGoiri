** BATTLESHIPS GAME** ** HUNDIR LA FLOTA **

Welcome to my Battleships Game. This game was developed while doing the Data Analysis Bootcamp in Bilbao, as part of my training in Python. 

The game is the classic Battleships Game, where you have to sink the boats of the enemy float by shooting in a matrix of 10x10. In this version, you can only play against the machine
but hopefully soon, I will be able to develop the code to play with a friend. 

Moreover, the ships are placed randomly, both for the player and the machine. It is a plan to update the programme so you will be able to place your ships as you wish.

Mechanics of the game are simple, in a matrix of 10x10 you have columns named with letters (A-J), and the lines named with numbers (1-10).
You will choose where to shoot your enemy in the grid, and if you hit the enemy, you have another chance. However, if you miss, then your turn ends and the machine will shoot randomly
to your board. The game ends when one of the players sink all the ships.
